## 📝 MÜZİK PROMPTU [§1.4 iskeletinin 160 sn · 20 × 8 sn hâli]

```
Instrumental score for a 160-second horizontal construction time-lapse: twenty 8-second shots, no end card.
No lyrics, no voice, no melody line, no traditional song structure. Low in the mix: it sits under the site sounds.

Genre: minimal ambient score, warm and patient, steady, then opening out.
Tempo: 120 BPM, strict and bar-aligned, four bars per 8-second shot.

Layers:
1. [shots 1-3] a soft dry pulse on the first beat of every bar
2. [shots 4-8] a low sustained bass note
3. [shots 9-13] sparse, delicate metallic tones, one at a time
   [shots 14-16] layers 1 and 3 step back and almost disappear; layer 2 holds alone, very low, leaving room for water
4. [shots 17-18] a wide warm string pad; layers 1 and 3 return softly
5. [shot 19] a high, open, calm chord — the widest moment of the score

Dynamic map across 160 seconds:
  0.0-1.5      silence — only the site's own sound (crane engine, wind, the river far below)
  1.5-24.0     layer 1 alone
  24.0-64.0    layer 2 enters
  64.0-104.0   layer 3 enters
  104.0-128.0  the score thins to layer 2 alone, very low — the water carries this section
  128.0-144.0  layer 4 enters, layers 1 and 3 return softly
  144.0-152.0  layer 5 enters, the score opens out, still quiet
  152.0-160.0  everything resolves and decays; the last 2 seconds carry only the river and soft water

Phone-speaker rule: every sub-bass element below 150 Hz is doubled by its own upper harmonics at roughly 100, 150 and
200 Hz so the fundamental is implied rather than required; the low end is never the only carrier of the weight.
```
