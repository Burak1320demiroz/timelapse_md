## 📝 MÜZİK PROMPTU [§1.4 iskeletinin 160 sn · 20 × 8 sn hâli]

```
Instrumental piece for a 160-second horizontal restoration time-lapse: twenty 8-second shots, no end card.
No lyrics, no voice, no traditional song structure until the last 16 seconds. It must sit under the site sounds until 144 s.

Genre: warm chamber-ambient score that becomes a small live open-air concert piece.
Tempo: 120 BPM, strict and bar-aligned, four bars per 8-second shot.

Layers:
1. [shot 8] a soft low pulse on the first beat of every bar
2. [shot 9] a low sustained bass note
3. [shot 10] a simple four-note motif on a single plucked or piano voice — the identity of the piece
4. [shots 11-13] a wide warm string pad; the motif returns a little fuller
   [shots 14-18] everything steps back to layer 2 alone, very low; the room is left to forest, footsteps, tuning and a
   gathering audience; in shot 15 the same four-note motif is heard once, distant, as if played on the stage in rehearsal
5. [shots 19-20] the motif becomes the opening of a small ensemble's live piece, heard from the stage with open-air
   reverb; in shot 20 its first strong rise, met by applause

Dynamic map across 160 seconds:
  0.0-56.0     silence — only the site's own sound (flowing water and pump hum from the first frame, mud, washing, stone,
               timber)
  56.0-64.0    layer 1 enters, very low
  64.0-72.0    layer 2 enters
  72.0-80.0    layer 3: the motif, first time
  80.0-104.0   layer 4 enters; the motif a second time, fuller
  104.0-144.0  the score drops to layer 2 alone, almost inaudible; at about 114 s the motif once, distant, from the stage
  144.0-152.0  the live piece begins from the stage on the motif, open-air reverb
  152.0-160.0  first strong rise; applause rises over it; both soften together in the last 1.5 seconds — no hard cut

Phone-speaker rule: every sub-bass element below 150 Hz is doubled by its own upper harmonics at roughly 100, 150 and
200 Hz so the fundamental is implied rather than required; the low end is never the only carrier of the weight.
```
