# LENS 3 — SITE
This lens answers one question: is it the same world across every cut, and is the site working
all the time, with a visible cause for every change? It judges everything on the site except the
structure — machines, huts, stacks, ground, routes, trees, water, wear, people — as a viewer who
never saw the plan; not the camera, rendering, light, passage of time, design, method or story.

INPUTS FOR THIS LENS
The video file and its frame grabs. No documents. S<n>_ilk, S<n>_orta, S<n>_son are the first,
middle and last frame of clip n, from S0. A seam pair is S<n>_son beside S<n+1>_ilk, one per cut.

BEFORE YOU SCORE — write these three lines first:

If a document is attached anyway, do not read it. If the grabs are missing, write GRABS NOT
SUPPLIED, audit the seams from playback and continue. Never stop for a missing grab or document.

RULES
- Watch the whole file once, then open the grabs, then audit seam by seam (Category 1).
- Seams are the measured CUT TIMESTAMPS. If END CARD is YES, the card is not a scene and the cut
  into it is not a seam: exclude it everywhere. If NO, the last clip is scored. A FINAL HOLD is
  not an idle stretch.
- Judge position against fixed landmarks beside the object, not the frame edge. Name objects by
  how they look ("yellow tracked excavator, long boom"), not by what you assume they are.
- WORK is a machine or a person visibly changing the site: digging, lifting, placing, pouring,
  pushing, carrying or driving material in or out, erecting, fixing. A still machine, a
  standing person, and moving shadows, clouds, water or dust are not work.
- Time passes at every cut: growth, a new task or a new machine across a cut can be what time
  produces. Inside a clip, every change needs a maker you can see.
- Score each defect once: fails to carry across a cut -> 1 and exactly one of 2-4 (1 counts
  seams; 2-4 judge object classes over the whole film) · no work anywhere for a span -> 5 ·
  moving but not working -> 6 · change with no work at its front or out of proportion -> 7 ·
  material or work state not carried on -> 8 · a triggered conditional takes what it names.
- A camera that moved, a shifted frame or anchor, text or a logo, how people and vehicles are
  rendered, render-look surfaces, and a rigid body that melts, morphs, resizes, passes through
  another or fades in or out WITHIN a clip belong to Lens 1. If you see it, write SEE LENS 1
  with the timestamp and do not lower any score here.
- Sun, shadow, season, sky, exposure, time rate, whether a cut reads as passed time (a seam
  where nothing changed), jump size, and the final seconds and hand-over belong to Lens 2. If
  you see it, write SEE LENS 2 with the timestamp and do not lower any score here.
- The structure itself — identity, growth, scale, piece-by-piece method and the agent on each
  element at its making step, task sequence, load path, a machine's fitness for its step,
  temporary items (crane, scaffold, hoist, formwork, props, fence, hut, ramp) erected and
  removed by visible steps, each element's material source, and whether an object or person is
  meant to be on site at all — belongs to Lens 4. If you see it, write SEE LENS 4 with the
  timestamp and do not lower any score here. While a temporary item stands, whether it stays
  the same object in the same place is judged here.
- Promise, final beat, opening hook, loop, scene variety, cover, sound bed and disclosure belong
  to Lens 5. If you see it, write SEE LENS 5 with the timestamp and do not lower any score here.
- FINDINGS list defects only. A continuity finding has two timestamps: the last moment the
  thing was one way, the first moment it was another (m:ss.s -> m:ss.s). Delete one without.
- NOT VISIBLE / NOT VERIFIABLE: if the media does not let you decide a point, write NOT
  VISIBLE or NOT VERIFIABLE for that point and leave it unscored. Neither is a zero.
- EVIDENCE RULE. A category score above 7 requires at least one timestamp (m:ss.s) and the
  visible thing at that timestamp that shows the category holding. "No defect found" is not
  evidence. Without it, the highest score for that category is 7.
- Timestamps are m:ss.s on this file. Do not write an AVERAGE line.

## CATEGORY 1 — THE SEAM AUDIT ⭐
At every measured cut, is the incoming world the same as the outgoing one, changed only the way
passing time would change it? Put S<n>_son beside S<n+1>_ilk, list everything that differs,
confirm each difference in playback around the cut, and classify it:
  INTENDED  what time would produce: more structure, work advanced, material used up, other
            season, light or weather, a machine that left or arrived by a route
  DEFECT    what time would not produce: an object moved with no one to move it, changed in
            colour, type or count, appeared where nothing could bring it, vanished unremoved
One SEAM TABLE row per seam. An empty list is NO CHANGE: write SEE LENS 2; it is not a defect
here. Score the ratio of seams that carry at least one DEFECT.
  10  every seam audited, none carries a DEFECT; a row names an object identical across its pair
  7   one seam with one minor DEFECT  ·  5  one noticeable DEFECT at one seam, or minor at two
  3   DEFECTs at two or more seams, fewer than half  ·  0  DEFECTs at half the seams or more

## CATEGORY 2 — MACHINE IDENTITY AND THE SAME PLANT
Is every machine the same machine each time it appears, and does the machine that starts a job
carry it on until it is done? For each machine in more than one clip compare type, size against
nearby objects, colour and configuration (crane head and jib, boom, tracks or wheels,
attachment, cab side); a tower crane's base never moves (its erection and climbing are Lens 4).
Defects: type, colour, size or configuration changes across a cut · a job taken over by a different
machine with no hand-over · plant already in position where no route could bring it, or where a
vanished machine stood · plant mid-cycle at one clip's end, absent at the next clip's start, its
job unfinished · a new set of machines every clip (re-dressed, not worked). INTENDED: a new
machine for a new task where a route reaches; a machine gone once its job is done.
  10  every repeated machine and every job holds; one machine named identical at two timestamps
  7   one minor detail drifts on one machine, once  ·  5  one machine changes, or one job does
  3   changes on two or more machines or cuts  ·  0  a different set of machines at most cuts

## CATEGORY 3 — OBJECTS, GROUND AND ROUTES CARRY OVER
Do standing objects, ground and ways in stay where they were, changing only as a crew, weather
and traffic would change them?
- Objects: huts, containers, fences, barriers, stacks, spoil piles, pipe runs, cable drums,
  skips. Lay all S<n>_orta grabs side by side to catch one that leaves at a seam and returns
  later. Defects: teleports · disappears and returns · changes count · a stack changes material.
- Ground is a memory (mud, ruts, spoil, dust, puddles): it carries and evolves, or it resets.
  Defects: ruts gone with no resurfacing · mud clean with no dry spell · puddles in a new place
  each clip · dirty in one shot, swept in the next. Standing water keeps to the same low ground.
- Routes: road, gate, ramps and haul routes keep place and line. Defects: a route changes · a
  gate moves · a ramp switches side · traffic from a direction no route serves, or on an unbuilt one.
  10  objects, ground and routes all carry; one of them named holding at two timestamps
  7   one minor object or ground patch resets once  ·  5  one noticeable object, ground or route failure
  3   two or more failures, or a route or gate moves  ·  0  the site is laid out afresh at most cuts

## CATEGORY 4 — VEGETATION, WATER AND WEAR
Do trees and water keep count, place and shape, and does the site look more used as the film
goes on? Three trees stay three; a river keeps its line and crossings. Seasonal foliage change is
INTENDED; a tree that moves, multiplies, vanishes or changes kind is a DEFECT. Wear: ground more
churned, edges more cluttered, surfaces more stained, spoil heaps larger, until a visible
clean-up. Judge wear from the whole film, not one seam. Defects: equally new in every shot ·
tidier with no clean-up seen · clutter reset to zero at a cut · surfaces losing their staining.
  10  trees and water hold and wear builds; a tree named at two timestamps, wear early and late
  7   one tree or patch of wear resets once  ·  5  a tree moves or multiplies, water moves, clutter resets
  3   two or more failures, or equally new in every shot  ·  0  trees rebuilt each shot, or site gets newer

## CATEGORY 5 — A WORKING FRONT, NO IDLE STRETCH
In every clip, is there one place where work is happening now, and does the site work the whole
clip? Name each clip's front in one phrase (the top slab, the pit floor, the tunnel mouth) or
NONE. Find its longest span with nothing on site working (light and weather may move), as
m:ss.s-m:ss.s and as a fraction of the clip between its cuts: under 1/6 no defect · 1/6-1/3
minor · 1/3-1/2 breaks the working site · over 1/2 parked · whole clip fails. Defects also: a
front where nothing moves · a front that moves between first and last frame with no work
joining them · a crane holding a load it never moves · still plant everywhere during heavy work
· busyness that ignores the work (as many machines at first excavation as at the peak). Idle
opening or close -> C5; night or slow clip -> C4. Tie-break with Lens 4: if the idle machine is
the missing agent of an element being made at that moment, write SEE LENS 4 with the timestamp and do
not lower this score for it.
  10  every clip has a named front with work and no idle span over 1/6; one front timestamped
  7   one clip idle 1/6-1/3, or one front hard to name  ·  5  one clip idle 1/3-1/2, or with no front
  3   one clip over half idle, or over 1/3 in two or more  ·  0  a whole clip idle, or most parked

## CATEGORY 6 — REAL MACHINE CYCLES
Is every machine that moves doing its real, repeating job with a result where it works? The
excavator digs, swings, dumps, returns; the hook rises, slews, lowers, releases; the pump boom
feeds the pour; the tipper backs, tips, leaves; the dozer pushes material; the roller makes
overlapping passes. Decorative: a truck crossing edge to edge without loading, tipping or
stopping · a bucket that never touches ground or dumps · a dozer pushing nothing · a vehicle
circling · a cycle that never completes or leaves no result. Loops: a crane slewing left, right,
left on one arc · a motion much shorter than the clip repeated identically through it (see CLIP
LENGTH ON FILE) · a pose that snaps back · the same pose in all three grabs while playback shows
motion. Compaction, curing and inspection are not aimless. A machine that never moves -> 5.
  10  every moving machine completes real cycles; one full cycle named with start and end
  7   one short decorative move or unfinished cycle  ·  5  one clear aimless motion or visible loop
  3   aimless or looped motion in two or more clips  ·  0  most motion is decorative or looped

## CATEGORY 7 — CAUSE, EFFECT AND WORK RATE
Does the site's work, taken as a whole, explain what changes — each change at the front where
the work is, while it is there, in proportion to it? At each change in the CLIP TABLE look at
the site at that moment: the slab grows where the pump works; the berm rises where the crawler
pushes; the pit deepens where the excavator digs; the heap grows where trucks tip. Count lifts,
loads or passes in the clip against pieces or volume placed: more pieces than lifts is a
defect. Defects: change at one front while all the work is at another · a pit, heap, cut or fill
that changes with no plant at it · growth far beyond the visible plant (one excavator empties a
pit, one crane adds several floors in a clip) · frantic activity with no result · a burst in one
instant the steady work around it does not explain. Judge proportion, not speed: at {SPEED}x
motion looks faster than it was. Growth across a cut is time passing when the plant and front
that would make it are on both sides. An agent missing from one element's making step (an empty
hook while a beam fixes itself) is Lens 4.
  10  every change in a clip has proportionate work at its front; one change and its work timestamped
  7   one small change whose work is unclear  ·  5  one clear change with no work at its front, or one burst
  3   the same in two or more clips  ·  0  the site changes by itself; work and change are unrelated

## CATEGORY 8 — MATERIAL FLOW AND PICKING UP WHERE IT LEFT OFF
Does material move through the site the way a crew moves it, and does every clip open mid-work
where the last one left off?
- Flow through the site: a delivery arrives by a route, is unloaded to a stock or to the work,
  and the truck leaves; spoil lands on a heap or leaves by truck; stocks are refilled by
  deliveries, not by themselves. Defects: a stack or heap that appears or refills inside a clip
  with no delivery · deliveries never used · spoil that goes nowhere (the pit deepens, no heap
  grows, no truck leaves). Whether each element draws on a visible source is Lens 4.
- Picking up: S<n+1>_ilk shows the site already working, at the front S<n>_son left or the next
  one, the material state carried on. Defects: a clip that opens with every machine still, then
  all start together · a front unrelated to where the last clip ended.
  10  deliveries, stocks and spoil move through the site; every clip opens mid-work; one timestamped
  7   one stock refills unexplained, or one clip opens a moment still  ·  5  one clear flow or pick-up failure
  3   two or more such failures  ·  0  material never flows; every clip restarts from parked

## CONDITIONAL CATEGORIES
Not triggered: write NOT TRIGGERED — no score, no average. Triggered: scored, EVIDENCE RULE too.

**C1 — PEOPLE ON SITE.** Open only if people are visible in any clip.
Do the people work, and do crew size and behaviour follow the work across the cuts? Density and
behaviour, not identity: more people at heavy work, few at first excavation; hands on tools and
materials; loads carried somewhere. Defects: the same crowd in every shot whatever the work ·
aimless walking · standing still all clip · hammering the air, gestures touching nothing · motion
resetting on a loop · a crew gone at a cut, its work unfinished · people on an unbuilt route.
10 crews work and follow the work, one task timestamped · 7 one idle or vague figure · 5 one
clear aimless, frozen or air-working crew · 3 the same in two or more clips · 0 set dressing

**C2 — MORE THAN ONE VANTAGE.** Open only if the film shows the site from more than one
intended camera position (a drift belongs to Lens 1).
Do the machines, objects and ground seen from the second vantage match the main one's in the
same period? A different set is a different site. Across a vantage change, compare shared objects.
10 shared objects match, one named from both vantages · 7 one minor mismatch · 5 one clear
mismatch · 3 two or more · 0 the vantages show different sites

**C3 — SNOW OR STANDING WATER.** Open only if snow or standing water shows in two or more clips.
Does it arrive, sit and leave in physical order, in the same low ground, and leave traces (mud,
meltwater, staining, wet ground) in the next clip? Defects: snow gone at a cut onto dry clean
ground with no thaw · a pond that moves · water on high ground · a flood with no mark. Its life
is scored here, not in 3 or 4. Whether the season fits is Lens 2.
10 ordered arrival, sitting and departure with traces, timestamped · 7 one trace missing ·
5 one clear reset · 3 two or more · 0 snow and water ignore the ground throughout

**C4 — SLOW SEASON OR NIGHT CLIP.** Open only if a clip shows night, storm, deep winter or slowing.
Work may slow but must not stop: fewer trips, a shorter cycle, one machine instead of three, and
at least one growth event linked to visible work. Zero work with nonzero growth fails. That
clip's idle span and unexplained growth are scored here, not in 5 or 7. Darkness is Lens 2.
10 every slow clip slows with linked growth, timestamped · 7 one thin but linked · 5 one stops
and nothing grows · 3 one stops while growth continues · 0 every slow clip stops while it grows

**C5 — OPENING AND CLOSING CLIPS.** Open only if the first clip begins with the site idle, or
the last scene clip shows the work ended.
Opening: work starts within the first quarter of the first clip. Closing: the site is cleared
(plant gone, stockpiles gone, surfaces finished), not abandoned (plant standing, material
stacked, nothing moving). These idle spans are scored here, not in 5. The right first task is
Lens 4; the final beat Lens 5; the final seconds and hold Lens 2.
10 early start, visible clearing, timestamped · 7 one minor lag or leftover · 5 a slow start
or one abandoned item · 3 both, or plant left standing · 0 idle opening and abandoned close

Verdict lines are independent of the scores; a bad verdict stands even when scores are high.
REBUILT EVERY SHOT: a viewer comparing any two clips would conclude the surroundings were built
separately for each shot. PARKED: taken as a whole, the structure grows while the site around
it mostly sits still. THE OBJECT THAT PROVES IT: one object on the site — not the structure, not
the distant background — identical at two timestamps in different clips; NONE is legitimate
and the most damaging thing this report can say, so do not invent one. THE GROWTH NOBODY MADE:
the clearest growth inside a clip with no work at that spot; the most important line under the
second verdict. THE MOST PURPOSELESS ACTIVITY: the one machine or person whose motion is most
obviously fake, looped or pointless. NONE is always legitimate. QUESTIONS FOR THE DOCUMENT: up
to five, numbered, that only the plan can answer and that would change a finding — was an
object planned to be removed, a machine meant to be replaced, a route change intended, a
machine declared parked in a clip, a night clip declared to carry work. Ask; do not answer.
